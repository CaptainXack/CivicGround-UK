# CivicGround UK

Independent police-powers education for England and Wales. Not legal advice; no comprehensive professional legal review. The project name is provisional pending a trademark check.

## Publish
In Settings > Pages choose Deploy from a branch, main, /(root). After the first successful online visit the PWA can cache its core files for offline use. External statutory texts require internet access.

## Features
129 scenarios, searchable legislation summaries and official links, legal timelines, quizzes, case law, complaint drafting, accessibility and young people's rights. No accounts or analytics. Do not publish personal incident information in this public repository.

## Legal review
The content is educational and not exhaustive. Inherited entries need professional review. Source monitoring is a page-change signal only, not automatic legal verification. Review changes before updating guidance.
