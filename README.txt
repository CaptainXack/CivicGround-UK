CivicGround UK v4.0 | England and Wales | 26 November 2026
Independent legal education, not legal advice. Not professionally reviewed.

Retains 129 scenarios, interactive myths, timelines, courtroom pathways, local-voice text-to-speech, searchable legislation and official full-text links, dated amendment comparisons, ranks and roles, accessibility and young people's support. 42 power references, 39 glossary entries. Full statutory text and historical versions are on legislation.gov.uk, not bundled offline. No claim of exhaustive laws. Speech depends on available local device voices.

Open index.html in a browser. For install and offline caching, host folder contents at HTTPS or localhost (python -m http.server 8000). Load once and allow the service worker to install. External links need internet. No accounts, analytics or automatic sending. Bookmarks and reading preferences persist locally; drafts do not.

Optional monitoring: run monitor_sources.py. Source page hashes indicate possible changes, not legal verification. Verify current law, territorial extent, amendments and commencement with official sources and qualified advice. The inherited legal entries have not all been reverified in this release.
